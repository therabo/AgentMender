# ADR-0018: Complete Session Removal, Projection Consistency and Maximum Safe Cleanup

Status: Accepted product contract and fail-closed assessment direction (2026-11-02).
M8.2.1 is OPEN. No complete-removal Desktop mutation profile is admitted.
Independent Sol/high `context_review` accepted this assessment boundary and its
property requirements before implementation. Exact provider mutation, GUI
coordination and restart acceptance remain separate review gates.

## Decision

Product removal requires independent primary and canonical catalog absence,
complete supported projection absence, reload/restart/fresh-discovery verification,
collateral preservation and maximum safe artifact cleanup. Every supported surface
has an exact profile/root/target binding and immediate, reload, restart and fresh
discovery checks. Empty/partial inventories and missing observations cannot prove
completeness. Unknown is not NotRelevant. Synthetic tests are not GUI acceptance.

Any visible or reconstructing residual prevents Removed and RemovedWithResiduals.
Technical residuals require proven inability to reconstruct supported projections
or intact primary state. Unknown risk in either domain prevents success. M3 health
meanings remain unchanged.

Remove every supported, safe, exclusively attributable unit. A unit may be an exact
row/record in a shared container. Preserve unrelated records, keys and required
ordering/bytes; never delete the whole container. Shared, ambiguous, unknown and
unsupported units remain protected. Cwd, filename substrings, size and confidence
percentages grant no ownership. Derive coverage from unique exact units and checked
measurable-byte sums. Coverage is diagnostic, never destructive authority.

Preserve M8.2 v2 plan/report/journal bytes, digests and replay meanings. Add a pure
product assessment, separate from execution authority. Present historical success
as primary cleanup with complete provider/UI removal explicitly unverified.
No M8.2 receipt supplies missing projection/restart/maximum-cleanup evidence.

### Future destructive contract

Before adding effects, a separately versioned manifest/plan must freeze all primary,
required projection and additional supported safe actions, exact record preimages,
retained dispositions, app/binary/root identities, coordination effects, ordering
and restart postconditions. Reuse the existing independent approval, transaction,
recovery and consumed DurableIntent. Old v2 grants authorize no new index/cache
mutation or app effect. No generic SQL/JSON/JSONL/cache port or parallel authority
store is allowed. Newly discovered units are retained and require replanning.

Coordinate Desktop before persistent projection mutation. A live Map/renderer can
rewrite deleted records; native rollout/SQLite locks do not close this boundary.
Prefer proven native invalidation; otherwise bind graceful shutdown, verified
termination and any permitted relaunch to approval. Never terminate arbitrary
processes, silently restart an app or launch an initially closed real app merely
for verification. Isolated future-launch acceptance must establish reappearance.

Review ordering against every crash point: durable intent, exact app coordination,
all preimage/fence checks, required projections, primary/catalog and extra cleanup,
permitted restart, fresh per-surface verification, durable product outcome.
M8.2 payload-first ordering is not inherited without cross-store analysis.
Interrupted projection mutation/restart means incomplete durable knowledge.
Current reconciliation stays read-only: no new targets, whole-store restoration,
automatic retry or reconstructed dispatch authority.

## Relationship to ADR-0017

ADR-0017 remains the accepted historical primary-cleanup contract and its exact
adapter remains available. This decision supersedes using that weaker result as
the current product complete-removal claim. It does not invalidate M8.2, broaden
its executor, rewrite receipts or claim forensic erasure. Native M8.1 and Claude
M9 blockers remain unchanged.

## Consequences and alternatives

Relabeling primary success would conceal ghosts; deleting caches or broad JSON
subtrees would risk collateral damage; transferring stable CLI authority to a
different Desktop profile would bypass compatibility. All are rejected.
Truthful incomplete assessment permits historical reclaim without a false GUI
promise. Missing evidence keeps M8.2.1 OPEN; it does not prove provider impossibility.

## Validation

Generated properties reject visible/unknown-risk residuals, missing required
surfaces/phases, foreign identities, incomplete inventories, unapproved/duplicate/
shared destructive units, skipped safe units and dishonest coverage. Positive
synthetic witnesses include every phase. Legacy replay/digests retain historical
meaning; CLI distinguishes primary success from product completeness.
Structural guards reject whole-container deletion, substring ownership, generic
mutation ports, broad process termination and unverified product success.

Full local gates and exact-final-commit three-OS CI are required. GUI acceptance is
a separate isolated opt-in gate; synthetic success cannot replace it. M8.3, M10
and natural-language implementation do not start.
